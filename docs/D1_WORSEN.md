## Release check of S1 (23-10-2026, `evaluation/d1_check.py`)

As registered: 50 random pairs flagged by S1 as the website would show them (website diseases, approved drugs neither
indicated nor already contraindicated for the disease in PrimeKG; 400 random diseases, seed 23, then 50 pairs from
their 76,411 flags), each with its current US label (openFDA, set id → DailyMed URL). Wrong = no section of the
current label (contraindications, boxed warning, warnings and precautions, adverse reactions) names the disease, a
direct broader term, or a condition it consists of. Gate: ≤ 5 % wrong, i.e. at most 2 of 50.

**S1 flags 191 drugs per website disease on average** (most with «contraindicated in 1 related disease, indicated in
0»). The pairs were checked in the sampled order; the first three were opened on DailyMed and are wrong, so the gate
cannot be met and the check stopped there. The next nine with a label were read from the openFDA sections (the same
SPL text) and none names the disease either:

**Decision: S1 is not released as a «May worsen this disease» section** (P56). The rule beats «contraindicated
somewhere» against PrimeKG (10.6 % vs 8.5 %), but as a per-disease list it flags about 190 drugs, most of them on the
strength of a single contraindication in one related disease, and the current labels do not support the flags.
Its existing use is unchanged: discarding risky candidates from the lists (P15). Not computed, as moot once the
release failed: the share of flags explained by confounding by indication, and recall of all PrimeKG
contraindications.
