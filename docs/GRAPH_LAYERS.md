# Graph layers: the internal graph and the publishable one

Generated by `py -3 layers.py report` from `data/graph/internal/manifest.json`. The ranking model and the website read only `data/prep` and the relations listed at the end (frozen in `resources/model_relations.txt`, checked by `tests/test_model_relations.py`). Every other layer is in the internal graph for exploration and future uses; a layer enters the model or the website only through a registered test (`docs/IMPROVEMENT_PLAN.md`).

Redistribution: **yes** = in the publishable graph (`py -3 layers.py export`, each layer under its own licence); **check** = licence or terms to confirm with the creators before publishing; **no** = internal only.

Nodes: 161,859 (157,822 core, 4,033 ATC class, 4 pathogen class).
