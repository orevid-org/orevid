candidates met their primary endpoint less often (65.9 %) than the drugs ranked 1–25 that the website did not show
(81.3 %). Much of that gap comes from approved indications, whose trials are the successful ones that led to approval

P38 (the shared-genes line behaves like a selection of drugs used in comparable diseases, not like biology). Caveats:
posted results are a biased sample (positive trials and industry trials post more), «met» is any primary analysis with
p < 0.05, and the pairs are few (126 shown).
