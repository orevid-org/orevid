  diseases and 47 of 645 external diseases. GenCC is about Mendelian disease, as expected in the registration.

  candidiasis, angiosarcoma, Rosai-Dorfman disease and POEMS syndrome have 0 supported genes, Angelman syndrome 1), so
  G1 could not have changed them, as expected.

  adopted, so they were not computed.

  the licence (CC0, attribution requested) but not built yet.
