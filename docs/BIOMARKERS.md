## Rules tried and rejected

**Gram stain (14-10-2026, proposal in STATUS).** Two rows with a closed drug list (`drugs:` rule type in
`web/build.py` `marker_rules`): «gram-negative bacterial infections» (36327) without the antibacterials that act
only on gram-positive bacteria (glycopeptides, lipoglycopeptides, daptomycin, oxazolidinones, streptogramins,
fidaxomicin, mupirocin, retapamulin, fusidic acid), and «gram-positive bacterial infections» (38057) without
aztreonam and the polymyxins. **Criterion (b) failed: 14 flagged pairs are approved indications in the data.** The
ontology lists as more specific forms of gram-negative infections diseases such as syphilis, yaws, typhus, chlamydia
infections and even conjunctivitis, and the data record fusidic acid, linezolid or vancomycin as indicated for some
of them. The inheritance through the hierarchy is too broad for a drug-class rule here, so the rows were removed.
A version limited to the two pages without inheritance would change nothing today (neither page shows such a drug).

**Antifungals and antibacterials by class (same day, analysis only).** The antifungal on the gram-positive page
(amphotericin B, a direct extension) comes from «Actinomycetales infectious disease», for which PrimeKG records
amphotericin B as indicated (probably through mycetoma, which can be bacterial or fungal): a source error. Across
the hierarchy, 0 bacterial-infection pages show an antifungal, but 30 antibacterials appear on fungal-infection
pages; a class rule («no antibacterials on fungal infections») would also remove trimethoprim–sulfamethoxazole for
pneumocystosis, its standard treatment (Pneumocystis is a fungus). Not applied.
