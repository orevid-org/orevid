## Results of the checks (09-11-2026, by hand)

- **F1a check 1: 17 of 20 labels right (criterion ≥ 18): not passed.** The three wrong labels come from the name route
  matching a phenotype term to a disease that is a form of the page disease (bipolar disorder for major affective
  disorder), a related disease (rheumatoid arthritis for juvenile idiopathic arthritis) or another disease that shares
  a generic phenotype name (Diamond-Blackfan anemia through «congenital hypoplastic anemia»).
- **F1a check 2: 3 of 20 unlabelled extensions should have been labelled (criterion ≤ 2): not passed.** Panuveitis
  (Vogt-Koyanagi-Harada), hypertrichosis (Rabson-Mendenhall) and intermittent claudication (arteriosclerosis) are
  features, but they are not among the current HPO annotations of those pages under the exact match.
- **F1a is not released.** Neither threshold is tuned after seeing the sample; a new route would need a new
  registration and a new sample.
- **F1b: 20 of 20 conditions match ClinicalTrials.gov: passed.** A first attempt used the condition text of the Open
  Targets records (amendment (2)); it did not match ClinicalTrials.gov in at least 8 of the same 20 trials (it adds
  conditions from the title or description, e.g. NCT06044337 «systemic lupus erythematosus», NCT00490113 «primary
  hyperoxaluria»; the first sample is kept in `data/eval/F1_first_sample_OT_text.md`). The registered source was then
  used: the conditions as registered, fetched from the ClinicalTrials.gov API v2 through the NCT ids
  (`py -3 -m sources.ctgov conditions`, 32,056 trials), and the same 20 trials were checked again. The check also shows
  why F1b matters: on «paraganglioma» the celecoxib phase-1 trial is registered for glioma (NCT02115074).
- **F1c** (no check required): released, the note on merged MONDO terms and the links to the page disease's HPO
  phenotypes that have their own page.
