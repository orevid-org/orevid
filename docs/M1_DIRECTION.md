## Reading (23-10-2026)

**By the registered rule, M1 does not validate the direction** (P58): on pQTL the ratio is 5.97 with a lower bound
of 1.10, but only 21 indication pairs fall among the directed pairs (30 required). Drug targets with a cis-pQTL
colocalising with a disease GWAS are few (226 drugs).

**Reported, not deciding: eQTL points the same way, with more data.** Indications are 4.5 times more frequent among
pairs where the drug's action agrees with the genetic direction (2.0–11.7; 106 indication pairs, 1,275 diseases),
also on the Open Targets approvals absent from PrimeKG (2.6, 1.2–7.6). Contraindications go the other way from the
expected risk signal (oppose ÷ agree 0.55, 0.34–0.88): drugs contraindicated in a disease are more often «agreeing»
ones, probably because contraindications concentrate on drugs used in related diseases (confounding by indication,
as in D1). Upper bounds are unbounded where the «oppose» arm has 0–2 positives in many resamples.

These eQTL numbers were seen before any new registration, so they cannot confirm themselves. The confirmation is
registered as **M1b** (`docs/IMPROVEMENT_PLAN.md`): the same ratio on approvals that appear after 26.06.

Earlier, cheaper version: `sources/gwas_direction.py` (`dir_gwas`, shared lead variant) was «suggestive but not
conclusive» and is in the graph, not scored (`docs/STATUS.md`); M1 uses real colocalisation (h4 ≥ 0.8) and cis QTL.

**Author's decision (23-10-2026, after this result):** the 30-positive minimum was set by the assistant without the author and does not stop a signal of this size; pQTL + eQTL is taken as the result (5.14, 2.49–12.34; 117 indication pairs) and the direction goes to a model test (M2, `docs/M2_DIRECTION_FEATURE.md`). M1b stays as an extra, prospective confirmation.
