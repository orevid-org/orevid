## Reading (23-10-2026)

The column changes almost nothing because it almost never applies: only 38 of the 335 benchmark test diseases and
74 of the 345 area test diseases have a drug with a directed target (the benchmark is mostly rare diseases; the
colocalisations come from GWAS of common diseases). Where it applies, the change is small and positive but within
noise: +0.004 (0.000 to +0.009) on the benchmark, +0.005 (−0.007 to +0.022) on the areas. Not adopted: the model
stays as it is. This measures whether the column helps the ranking on these tests, not whether the genetic
direction is informative: M1 showed it is (pQTL + eQTL, 5.1 times more indications among agreeing pairs). The next
step registered is to show it as information on the pages, not as a score.
