Generated by `py -3 -m evaluation.lincs_reversal`. Protocol: `docs/IMPROVEMENT_PLAN.md` (I4) and the module docstring. Data: Enrichr libraries `LINCS_L1000_Chem_Pert_Consensus_Sigs` and `Disease_Perturbations_from_GEO_up/down` (CREEDS).

## Coverage

- LINCS consensus signatures: 5,425 compounds; matched to graph drugs: **2,410**.
- Disease signatures: 840; mapped to one graph disease: 715 (human 504, mouse 192, rat 19; unmapped or ambiguous 125), covering **239 diseases**.
- Drug–disease pairs with both signatures: 575,990.
