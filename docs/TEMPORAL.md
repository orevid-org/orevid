## Reading of T2 (24-10-2026)

Dating drugs by their first approval removes most of the leakage the I1 design left (80 % → 11 % of PrimeKG indications undated). The claim holds (+0.10 at 2015, interval 0.08–0.13) but the I1 estimate (+0.15) was about a third too large, and the advantage on non-obvious uses is no longer shown. For drugs that did not exist at the cutoff, OREVID puts 8.6 % of their later uses in its top 100, about 7 times chance (the logistic model without popularity, 12.0 %). The preprint and the Method page should quote T2.
